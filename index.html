Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the coverage-check form, which is the primary entry point for visitors to determine eligibility and proceed to purchase. However, this mechanism is bleeding value due to unclear form field labels, ambiguous submit button text, and duplicate form instances on the same page, all of which create friction and confusion at the critical moment of engagement.

Voice of customer

What customers actually say

Gathered from third-party reviews, forums and video — the site’s own pages are excluded. Every quote was verified word-for-word against the page it links to; anything that could not be verified was discarded rather than paraphrased.

Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Unexpected price increases and hidden fees
Instead, I was hit with a surprise charge of $139 just a few months later, $40 more than expected, without any prior notice given that my rate was going up.
— gratefulglamper.com
Customers feel misled by undisclosed rate hikes and rental charges, eroding trust and increasing purchase anxiety.
Misleading claims about carriers and coverage
They said they would ship two Sims with the modem and I could pick whichever had the better coverage. Paid for the modem and plan, and received a Verizon modem and Sim.
— ie.trustpilot.com
Customers feel deceived when promised carrier flexibility but receive a locked device, increasing perceived risk of purchase.
Equipment not delivered or defective
Unfortunately, I still have not received my system.
— gratefulglamper.com
Shipping delays and faulty equipment undermine confidence in the product and service reliability.

Said after buying — these shape what the page must reassure

Impossible to reach human support
The worst service..impossible to talk to human support..all chat and text doesn't get anything solved.
— bbb.org
The lack of accessible human support frustrates customers and makes problem resolution seem hopeless, deterring potential buyers.
Billing errors and unauthorized charges
I bought the router outright, but 8 months later, I suddenly got charged $25/month rental fees.
— ie.trustpilot.com
Recurring billing mistakes and unauthorized charges create distrust and make customers wary of committing to the service.
Cancellation process is a nightmare
No one could tell me how to cancel. They just kept asking why I wanted to.
— dbakevlar.com
The difficult cancellation process traps customers and signals a lack of customer-centricity, discouraging sign-ups.
